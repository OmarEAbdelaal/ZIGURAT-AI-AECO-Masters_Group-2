## Intended use

**Primary.** Screening building-exterior images to estimate envelope composition —
Window-to-Wall Ratio and relative element counts — as a perception input to Group 02's
FMP Value Engineering decision-support system, at concept and early schematic design where
no BIM model of the subject building exists.

**Intended users.** Architects, cost consultants and BIM coordinators, working with a
qualified human reviewing every output.

**Out of scope.** Quantity take-off, cost certification, regulatory or code compliance,
BIM-vs-reality verification, structural assessment, and anything involving people.
Full statement: [`governance_checklist.md`](governance_checklist.md) §5.

## Factors

Performance varies materially with: **element scale** (instances under ~900 px² are
substantially less reliable), **class** (`door` is the weakest at recall 0.830),
**aspect ratio** (thin elements such as `louvre_screen` are found but boxed loosely),
**occlusion**, **glare**, and **façade typology** (trained on mid-rise contemporary
envelopes; heritage and vernacular are unmeasured).

## Metrics

Headline metric is **mAP50-95**, not mAP50, because the downstream VE read-out is computed
from box area and mAP50 is blind to a 30 %-oversized box.
Reasoning: [`problem_framing.md`](problem_framing.md) §3.

| | Precision | Recall | mAP50 | mAP50-95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Overall** | 0.975 | 0.946 | 0.948 | **0.879** |
| `window` | 0.980 | 0.951 | 0.985 | 0.951 |
| `curtain_wall` | 0.980 | 1.000 | 0.995 | 0.976 |
| `door` | 0.952 | 0.830 | 0.821 | 0.809 |
| `balcony` | 0.994 | 0.970 | 0.965 | 0.897 |
| `louvre_screen` | 0.971 | 0.979 | 0.971 | **0.764** |

> ⚠️ **Verification run on synthetic data.** These figures demonstrate that the pipeline
> works. They are not evidence of real-world performance and must not be quoted as such.

## Training data

120 procedurally generated façade elevations (96 train / 24 val), containing 2 578
labelled instances across 5 classes with a 13.8 : 1 imbalance, plus deliberately planted
unlabelled decoys. Generated by `src/synthetic_facades.py`, deterministic from `seed=42`.
Provenance and licensing: [`../data/README.md`](../data/README.md).

## Evaluation data

The 24-image validation split of the same distribution — same generator, disjoint seeds.
**This is a weakness worth stating plainly:** evaluating on the same synthetic
distribution the model trained on measures interpolation, not generalisation. The
`results/evidence/new_images/` pack uses held-out seeds and is a slightly better test;
neither substitutes for photographic data.

## Ethical considerations

No `person` or `vehicle` class exists, by design — data minimisation applied at the class
contract rather than bolted on afterwards. The model is structurally incapable of
identifying a person. No biometric processing. No client or employer imagery.
Full analysis: [`governance_checklist.md`](governance_checklist.md).

## Caveats and recommendations

1. **Do not deploy on real imagery without re-training.** The synthetic-to-real domain gap
   is large and the reported metrics will not survive it.
2. **Never treat pixel areas as square metres.** Uncalibrated single-image measurement is
   an unsolved problem, not an engineering detail.
3. **Report mAP50-95.** A stakeholder shown mAP50 0.971 for `louvre_screen` would conclude
   that class is solved; at mAP50-95 0.764 it is the model's worst.
4. **Fix the data before the model.** Every failure in
   [`error_analysis.md`](error_analysis.md) is a data gap. A larger model trained on the
   same gaps learns the same wrong cues with more capacity.
5. **Keep a human in the loop.** This screens. It certifies nothing.
